## Parts that come from elsewhere

The terms above cover EdSharp itself: the program, its scripts, and its
documentation. EdSharp also ships some things other people wrote, and each of
those keeps its own license. Using EdSharp is not affected by this; it matters
if you plan to reuse a part of it in your own work.

- The Universal Encoding Detector library, Ude.dll, is under the GNU Lesser
  General Public License. Its terms are in the file lgpl.txt, installed
  beside EdSharp.
- The 2htm text extraction tool lives in the Convert folder with its own
  license file.
- Pandoc, Python, Node, Ollama and the other tools EdSharp can fetch are not
  part of EdSharp. Each is installed separately under its own terms.
- The Hunspell library, the spelling dictionaries, and the other libraries
  EdSharp uses each carry their own notice in the package they came from.
