I use EdSharp for my complex text editing and I like it very much. I have a few suggestions for its improvement:

1. Add its own internal spell checker, instead of relying on MS Office (Word) one. That way, we don't need to have MS Office installed in order to spell check a file in EdSharp. And if the integrated spell checker can report misspelled words to the screen reader while reading the document, that will be even better - I prefer to spell check my documents while reading them line by line, and not in the spell check window.

2. Add a combo-box in the "Save as" window, from witch we can choose the encoding of the output text file (incl. saving without BOM for UTF-8 files).

Thanks much in advance!
