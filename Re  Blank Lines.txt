Yes, in EdSharp, C-like syntax is supported for control characters in strings, e.g., \n for new line, \t for tab, and \f for form feed.  So you can replace single line breaks with doubles by putting \n in the search string and \n\n in the replace string of the Control+R command.  Note that only affects hard line breaks, not lines that word wrap.
