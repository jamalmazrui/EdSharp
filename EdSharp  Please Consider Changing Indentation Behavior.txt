I hope this is easy for you to do. Here's something I'd truly like EdSharp to do when you press Tab on the current line, or when you press Tab on a selected set of lines. The same for Shift Tab. I want it to check if the lines are between indent levels. Right now, for example, my indent levels are set to two spaces. This means that if I accidentally have a line that I indented to one space, and I press the Tab key, that line will be indented to three spaces. It unconditionally moves lines by indent level. Can it check that and try to move lines to the appropriate level? If I have a majority of lines correctly indented to level one, and I press tab on a selection of lines, and one of the lines is between level one and level two, I want it to correct that problem for me.
