# Pie chart example

This is the shape the matching snippet inserts. It names itself, describes
itself, and sets no theme, so Check Markdown, Alt+F9, reports nothing.

```mermaid
pie title Share by category
    accTitle: Share by category
    accDescr: First takes 45 percent, Second 30 percent, and Third the remaining 25 percent.
    "First" : 45
    "Second" : 30
    "Third" : 25
```
